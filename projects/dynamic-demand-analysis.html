Product-design research · Project lead

AI-Assisted Dynamic User-Demand Analysis

A structured workflow combining large-scale review analysis and visual analytics to identify changing automotive user needs and translate them into traceable product-design priorities.

Period
November 2024–May 2025
Data
2,000 reviews · 3,674 sentence records
Methods
Feature, sentiment, and time-series analysis
Case
BYD Song PLUS

Research question

How can fragmented and time-varying online feedback become evidence that product designers can inspect and use?

My role

I led the research and analytical workflow. Collaborators supported data collection; I analyzed the review corpus, developed the analysis logic, constructed the three-agent workflow, and translated results into a visual analytics prototype.

Workflow

  • Segment reviews into sentence-level records.
  • Cluster feedback around product features and usage concerns.
  • Estimate sentiment and examine change over time.
  • Use a three-agent workflow to organize analysis and recommendations.
  • Present evidence in a traceable interface for design interpretation.

Case findings

The BYD Song PLUS case highlighted recurring concerns around legroom, accommodation of different body sizes, space under full occupancy, and ergonomic support.

Scope and limits

The workflow was compared with a standalone LLM through a case-based analysis. The project demonstrates traceability and design usefulness in one product context; it does not claim a broad benchmark across domains or models.
